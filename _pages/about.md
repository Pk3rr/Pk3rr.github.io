My name is **Bilal** and I'm passionate about cybersecurity, specializing in penetration testing. The goal of this website is to share my knowledge and learn from others.

My passion for computing began in high school, when I had the opportunity to study a subject related to information technology. In that course, I excelled in both practical and theoretical activities, which allowed me to develop skills in areas such as web development with HTML, scripting, and projects ranging from video games to logic calculators.

This initial experience was key to discovering my vocation, leading me to pursue specialized vocational training, marking the start of a path full of learning and professional growth.

Today, I specialize in cybersecurity, with a particular interest in penetration testing and Red Team strategies. I have completed a specialization in Cybersecurity in Technological Environments, where I gained advanced knowledge in areas such as:

- Memory dump analysis
- Malware analysis
- Web hacking
- Wireless hacking
- Network infrastructure pentesting

Beyond my academic training, I have dedicated myself to self-learning in preparation for recognized cybersecurity certifications, such as **eJPTv2**, **eCPPTv3**, **OSWP**, and **OSCP+**. My goal is to strengthen my skills and secure a position as a pentester.

I have also worked on projects such as developing a script to automate security audits, and creating and configuring virtual machines using **Ansible** and **Terraform**.
